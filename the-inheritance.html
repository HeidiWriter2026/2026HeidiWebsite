Inlet Beach Novels

The Inheritance

Three bitterly estranged sisters inherit a run-down beach house from a relative they don’t remember, in a town they’ve never visited.

The sisters gather for the first time in twelve years with very different intentions. The oldest sister, Maureen, wants a private retreat for only her immediate family. The middle sister, Lydia, sees a quick sale as the perfect solution for her overwhelming debt. Tyra, the youngest, hopes for forgiveness for the mistake that splintered the family and caused the estrangement.

And over the course of a crisp Pacific Northwest spring, these three women will attempt to repair the house and rebuild their family.
